# The Columbus Axis

**Gamified RPG · TCG · 3D City Builder** of the real residential, commercial, office, and industrial market of Central Ohio.

Become the King of the Buckeye State. Collect cinematic property cards, raise steel on a living Three.js map of Columbus, earn **Axis Coin**, outbid rival developers, and let ownership — not sliders — drive the prediction tape.

## Play

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

1. **New empire** — 1,000 Axis Coin and a starter hand.
2. Select a card, then **Place** it on its neighborhood (or click the tile).
3. **Collect rent** each season. Rivals (Scioto Capital, Buckeye Land) will bid.
4. **Draw** to expand the hand. **Axis Market** to trade the tape your buildings move.
5. Optional **note** (leverage). **Renovate** owned sites for more rent.
6. Survive the **cycle-6 rate shock**, then win by **60% Dominion**, the **urban core**, or **4,800 net worth**.

Empire state autosaves in `localStorage`.

## What ships

- **20 property cards** with original architectural art of real Central Ohio neighborhoods.
- **Living 3D city** — Scioto River, I-270, downtown fabric, landmarks, traffic, seasonal light.
- **Rival developers** who claim tiles; **hostile takeover** at 1.5× cost.
- **City-desk events** (gameday, polar vortex, fab slip, cargo records…).
- **Axis Market** — YES/NO tape biased by what you own.
- **Audio** — procedural trading-floor bed, unlocked on first gesture.
- **Win / lose** — Dominion, urban core, net worth, or receivership.

Neighborhood and submarket names are real. Every dollar figure and probability is fictional.
